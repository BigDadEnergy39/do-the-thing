F-Droid graphics go in this folder. Replace this README once the images exist.

Required / recommended (PNG or JPG):

  icon.png              512 x 512   App icon shown in the F-Droid client.
  featureGraphic.png    1024 x 500  Banner at the top of the listing (optional
                                    but strongly recommended).
  phoneScreenshots/     At least 1, ideally 3-5 screenshots. Filenames sort
                        alphabetically, so name them 01.png, 02.png, etc.

Notes:
- The build does NOT depend on these; F-Droid will publish without them, but
  the listing looks bare. Add at least one screenshot before submitting.
- Keep everything here free of third-party logos/marks you don't have rights to.
- These are listing assets only — they are not bundled into the APK.
